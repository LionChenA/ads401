# Question Backlog — Survey Instrument Design (ADS401 Group A)

> Working list of open questions for the survey design phase. These are **instrument-design
> questions** — the study's research questions (RQ1–3) live in the web report. Recorded
> 2026-10-05; research intentionally **not started yet**.

## A. Scale strategy

- **Q1.** Do academically validated scales exist for AI-related constructs, as opposed to
  traditional technology scales — and which tradition applies to us?
  *(User's opening question, 2026-10-05. Decomposes into: which constructs have validated
  instruments; norms for adapting them; whether our N≈20–30 can support scale use at all.)*
- **Q2.** Validated scale vs. custom items: is a full scale necessary, or is "adapt a validated
  scale + report Cronbach's α" the defensible middle given our sample size?

## B. Scope & constructs

- **Q3. [BLOCKING]** Scope of "AI" in the survey: **campus services only** (links to D-04) or
  **students' general AI use** (ChatGPT etc.)? This single fork rewrites items, sampling and the
  findings chapter. Our RQ draft assumed campus services; the census ambition says "AI usage"
  broadly. Must be settled first.
- **Q4.** Which constructs map to which RQ — awareness / adoption / barriers, plus attitude or
  trust? Which can we defensibly drop?

## C. Sampling & reach

- **Q5.** Census ambition: invite all ~50? What response-rate floor keeps claims
  census-adjacent (≥20? ≥30?)?
- **Q6.** Reach channels: cohort group chat, seminar slot, paper fallback — and what
  permissions each channel needs.
- **Q7.** How do we capture non-response (who didn't answer, why) — the robust-sampling marks
  depend on this.

## D. Ethics

- **Q8.** Required approval flow before fielding: module ethics process, information sheet +
  consent form — by what date?
- **Q9.** Anonymity vs. interview recruitment: how do respondents opt into follow-up interviews
  without being deanonymized (contact ticket at the end?).
- **Q10.** Social desirability: AI-for-coursework touches academic-integrity norms — how do we
  phrase items to reduce under-reporting?

## E. Instrument engineering

- **Q11.** Platform (WJX / MS Forms / Google Forms): anonymity vs. tracking trade-offs, XJTLU
  accessibility.
- **Q12.** Language: English, Chinese, or bilingual? What does translation/validation cost?
- **Q13.** Length budget (≤8–10 min?) → how many items fit; skip logic (unaware respondents
  skip the usage block).
- **Q14.** Pilot: who (3–5 classmates), by when (hard deadline ~15 Oct — Pres2 on 19 Oct needs
  pilot data), iterate on what?

## F. Analysis & linkage

- **Q15.** What analysis does this design license (descriptives, correlations, Cronbach's α) —
  and do we pre-register hypotheses (e.g., awareness ↔ adoption) for Pres3?
- **Q16.** Survey→interview linkage: sample interviewees by response patterns? Sequence —
  interviews first (inform items) or after (explain patterns)?

## Sequencing

- Before drafting items: **Q1–Q4** (literature research + group decision on Q3).
- Before fielding: Q5–Q13 + ethics flow (Q8).
- Hard clock: pilot by **~15 Oct**; fielding window **19 Oct – 2 Nov**; Pres3 **9 Nov**.
