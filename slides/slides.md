<div class="section-label">RESEARCH QUESTIONS</div>

<div class="deck-title" style="font-size: 1.5rem; margin-top: 1.3rem;">
  Three questions: know it, use it, and why (not)
</div>
<hr class="top-divider">

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-4 mt-2">
  <div class="rcard">
    <div class="content-h">Awareness &amp; Adoption</div>
    <b>RQ1</b>
    <div class="text-sm mt-1">To what extent are ADS401 students aware of and adopting XJTLU AI?</div>
  </div>
  <div class="rcard">
    <div class="content-h">Usage Patterns</div>
    <b>RQ2</b>
    <div class="text-sm mt-1">How do ADS401 students use XJTLU AI for their academic tasks?</div>
  </div>
  <div class="rcard">
    <div class="content-h">Adoption Factors</div>
    <b>RQ3</b>
    <div class="text-sm mt-1">Which perceived benefits, barriers, and differences vs external AI are associated with adoption and intentions to continue, try, or resume?</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="takeaway">RQ1–RQ2 ask <i>what</i> happens; RQ3 asks <i>why</i> — usefulness, ease of use and trust in play.</div>

<div class="section-label">RESEARCH METHODOLOGY</div>

<div class="deck-title" style="font-size: 1.45rem; margin-top: 1.15rem;">
  Survey first, interviews second — explanatory sequential design
</div>
<hr class="top-divider">

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-1">
<div>
  <div class="content-h">Cross-sectional survey</div>
  <ul class="text-sm" style="color: var(--ink);">
    <li>Frame: 50 enrolled → <b>4 researchers excluded → 46 invitees</b>, voluntary</li>
    <li>Four groups by past-4-week ADS401 AI use: <b>Both AI / Other AI only / No AI / XJTLU AI only</b></li>
    <li>Measures: awareness, ever-use, recent usage, usefulness, ease of use, trust, barriers, intentions</li>
    <li>Usefulness &amp; ease-of-use items are <b>exploratory</b>, context-specific — not the full validated TAM scales</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<div>
  <div class="content-h">Descriptive analysis plan</div>
  <ul class="text-sm" style="color: var(--ink);">
    <li>Counts &amp; percentages with per-item denominators</li>
    <li>Cross-tabulation across the four groups</li>
    <li>Rating summaries exclude N/A</li>
    <li>Paired comparison only where dual users rated the <b>same activity category</b>; "unable to compare" reported</li>
  </ul>
</div>
</div>

<div class="takeaway">46 invited, four usage groups, descriptive only — no causal claims.</div>
<hr class="bottom-divider">
<div class="slide-refs">Ivankova et al. (2006) doi:10.1177/1525822X05282260 · Chung et al. (2024) doi:10.26180/27915930 · Davis (1989) doi:10.2307/249008</div>
<div class="page-no">03 / 05</div>

<div class="section-label">FOLLOW-UP INTERVIEWS &amp; INTEGRATED ANALYSIS</div>

<div class="deck-title" style="font-size: 1.45rem; margin-top: 1.15rem;">
  Interviews explain what the survey finds
</div>
<hr class="top-divider">

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-1">
<div>
  <div class="content-h">Semi-structured interviews</div>
  <ul class="text-sm" style="color: var(--ink);">
    <li><b>Purposive sampling</b> among respondents who opt into follow-up contact</li>
    <li>About <b>4–6 students</b>, prioritising <b>Both AI</b> and <b>Other AI only</b> groups</li>
    <li><b>15–20 min</b> each: tool-choice motivations, adoption barriers, learning experiences</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<div>
  <div class="content-h">Thematic analysis &amp; integration</div>
  <ul class="text-sm" style="color: var(--ink);">
    <li>Code transcripts, develop themes (Braun &amp; Clarke)</li>
    <li>Connect themes to survey patterns (Fetters et al.)</li>
    <li>Outcome: explain adoption decisions → XJTLU AI improvements</li>
  </ul>
</div>
</div>

<div class="takeaway">The survey shows <i>how</i> students use AI services; interviews explain <i>why</i> they choose them.</div>
<hr class="bottom-divider">
<div class="slide-refs">Braun &amp; Clarke (2006) doi:10.1191/1478088706qp063oa · Fetters et al. (2013) doi:10.1111/1475-6773.12117</div>

<div class="section-label">MATERIALS · LIMITATIONS · NEXT STEPS</div>

<div class="deck-title" style="font-size: 1.45rem; margin-top: 1.15rem;">
  Honest boundaries, clear next steps
</div>
<hr class="top-divider">

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-1">
<div>
  <div class="content-h">Limitations (pre-registered)</div>
  <ul class="text-xs" style="color: var(--ink);">
    <li><b>Single-module sample</b> — no generalisation beyond participating ADS401 students</li>
    <li><b>Self-reported</b> behaviours &amp; perceptions</li>
    <li><b>Interview coverage</b> — G3/G4 may be under-represented</li>
    <li><b>Cross-sectional</b> — intentions, not causal or long-term effects</li>
    <li><b>Perception-based comparison</b> — not objective capability differences</li>
  </ul>
</div>
