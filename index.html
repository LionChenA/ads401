2 · Problem, motivation and research questions Draft — pending group confirmation

Context and users. AI is being integrated into everyday university life at XJTLU. Our target population is the 2026-intake ADS401 cohort (≈ 50 students) — a group that lives inside this integration and to whom we have direct access.

The evaluation problem. University AI services are built on an assumption of use, but adoption is not guaranteed. Rather than asking whether an AI system is "good", we investigate the gap between availability and actual use.

  • RQ1 (Awareness): Are 2026-cohort ADS401 students aware of the campus AI services available to them?
  • RQ2 (Adoption & use): Which services do they use, for which academic tasks, and how?
  • RQ3 (Barriers): What barriers explain non-use or abandonment?

Scope of claims. Findings will be bounded to the 2026 ADS401 cohort; with a ≈ 50-person population and a census-adjacent sample, we can make strong descriptive claims about this cohort but no general claims about XJTLU as a whole.

4 · System and evaluation design Draft sketch

  • Method (planned): anonymous online survey across the ≈ 50-student cohort (target ≥ 20 responses, census-adjacent) + 6–8 semi-structured interviews; mixed-methods.
  • Design constraint: cohort is too small for powered between-group comparisons — descriptive statistics, correlations and qualitative themes instead.
  • Sampling: sampling frame = the full cohort; non-response will be analysed and reported.
  • Ethics (planned): information sheet + informed consent, anonymity, minimal data collection; no raw participant data will appear on this site or in this repository, per module policy.

Full procedure, measures, materials (survey script, interview guide, consent form) to be added before Presentation 2 (19 Oct).
