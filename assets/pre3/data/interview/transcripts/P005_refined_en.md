### 2. Tool Preferences and Pain Points (CIT)

**[01:35] [Interviewer]:** So you use external AI relatively more. Compared to XJTLU AI, do you feel external AI or XJTLU AI is more practical?

**[Interviewee]:** I feel external AI is still better.

**[01:46] [Interviewer]:** In the future for ADS401, which type would you lean towards using?

**[Interviewee]:** Because I'm already used to external AI right now, it'll probably still be external AI. However, if XJTLU AI could directly summarize ADS401 materials—for example, if the instructor releases requirements or content and it could summarize them well—using that would definitely be more convenient.

**[02:14] [Interviewer]:** So if XJTLU AI had similar capabilities, you would come back and try XJTLU AI?

**[Interviewee]:** Yes, I would give it a try.

**[Interviewer]:** For external AI, are there areas that you find difficult to use or unsatisfactory?

**[02:31] [Interviewee]:** For instance, file upload limits. Also when it summarizes, it feels not very flexible and doesn't know this course well. Every time I go in, I have to open a new chat window and talk to it about this course all over again; it won't retain memory from last time, which is quite troublesome for me.

**[Interviewer]:** During your study in this course, did you try uploading course files to external AI?

**[Interviewee]:** Mm, yes.

**[02:57] [Interviewer]:** In that transfer process, or for external AI accessing internal XJTLU files (including this course), do you find it easy or difficult?

**[Interviewee]:** Extremely difficult, basically impossible to find directly. I have to upload them manually, so it's quite troublesome.

**[Interviewer]:** So for XJTLU AI, assuming it has that capability, would you consider it much more convenient than external tools?

**[Interviewee]:** It would be much more convenient.

**[03:24] [Interviewer]:** Understood. So that would also be a reason supporting your use of it—if XJTLU AI truly had this capability, you'd use it, right?

**[Interviewee]:** Yes, right.

### 3. Tool Expectations and Privacy Concerns

**[03:25] [Interviewer]:** Okay, thank you. Another question: what other capabilities would you hope XJTLU AI to have? You can reference external AI or your own experience.

**[03:43] [Interviewee]:** For coursework specifically: in the course I need to know the final credit criteria and my current progress towards meeting standards, which external AI definitely cannot do. Since it's a campus tool that can be tailored to the course, I hope it can explicitly tell me what I can do for this course, and my current status—such as which week we are in, what content has been covered, and what concepts I haven't mastered well. If it could mark those clearly, or if I could chat with it clearly about that, that would be great.

**[04:29] [Interviewer]:** Suppose XJTLU's AI took a step further, like an AI teaching assistant embedded directly inside pages like Learning Mall—

**[Interviewee]:** Wow, that would be awesome!

**[Interviewer]:** How would you evaluate this AI, or what features would you think it needs?

**[04:48] [Interviewee]:** Although I'd have a bit of concern—after all, as a student using AI, regarding how I use it, I don't want the university knowing too much (privacy/surveillance concerns). But I think it would provide more help for my coursework, like a little assistant. Every time I enter, without friction, I could just open it for this course, chat through everything, and wouldn't need to turn to other tools.

**[05:11] [Interviewer]:** In other words, you care about the relevance of this AI to the course content, while simultaneously keeping privacy in mind. Great, thank you, that covers our questions.
