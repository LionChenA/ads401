## 逐字稿（英中对照）

> 格式：每个发言先给英文翻译（EN），再给中文原文（CN）。说话人统一标注「访谈员 / 被访谈者」。

**访谈员**
> EN：This is our third interview. First, let's recall your understanding of XJTLU AI—do you remember what option you chose in the questionnaire back then?
> CN：这是我们的第三个采访。首先让你回忆一下你对西浦 AI 的了解，你还记得当时是怎么填写那个选项的吗？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Yes, I remember.
> CN：记得。

**访谈员**
> EN：Did you select whether you had used XJTLU AI or had any understanding of it?
> CN：你当时选的选项是有没有用过西浦 AI，还是有没有了解过它？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Not much understanding—neither used it nor understood it.
> CN：没有怎么了解，没有用过，也没有了解。

**访谈员**
> EN：Mainly still using external tools, right?
> CN：主要还是用外部的，对吧？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Right.
> CN：对。

**访谈员**
> EN：What was the primary reason you didn't use it?
> CN：你不使用它的主要原因是什么？

**被访谈者**
> EN：The primary reason for not using it is that external AI has been validated by a vast number of users and is reliable. Meanwhile, the course instructors never actively recommended XJTLU AI, so I don't know much about it and haven't really used it.
> CN：不使用的主要原因，就是外部的 AI 经过大量用户的验证是可靠的。而西浦的 AI 老师也没有主动去推荐它，所以我对它不了解，就没怎么用。

**访谈员**
> EN：By comparison, you trust external AI more, right?
> CN：相比较来说，你是更信任外部的 AI，对吧？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Right.
> CN：对。

**访谈员**
> EN：Have you tried using external AI for learning activities related to ADS401?
> CN：你有尝试用外部的 AI 去做一些跟 ADS401 相关的学习活动吗？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Yes.
> CN：有。

**访谈员**
> EN：Could you give an example?
> CN：可以举一下例子吗？

**被访谈者**
> EN：For instance, experimental methods taught in lectures—translating some course slides, plus understanding mathematical and statistical formulas.
> CN：比如说老师课上讲的一些实验方法，会有一些课件的翻译，加上数学公式这些的理解。

**访谈员**
> EN：Mainly around course knowledge acquisition and study assistance. Regarding XJTLU AI, you know relatively little about it. If external AI and XJTLU AI had similar, comparable capabilities, would you try to learn about or start using XJTLU AI?
> CN：主要还是围绕课程知识的学习或辅助。那对于西浦的 AI 来说，你是对它了解比较少的。如果外部 AI 和西浦 AI 有类似相同的能力，你会不会尝试了解或者开始使用西浦 AI？

**被访谈者**
> EN：If XJTLU AI were more convenient to use, I would try it.
> CN：如果西浦 AI 用起来更方便的话，会尝试用。

**访谈员**
> EN：When using external AI for ADS401, did you encounter any noticeable friction or inconvenience?
> CN：那你在把外部 AI 用在 ADS401 这门课的时候，有没有发现一些明显的、让你觉得不方便的地方？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Yes, it exists. For example, if you want it to teach you, you need to import the lecture slides into it, which takes a bit of time. Furthermore, external AI's general pedagogical style provides problem-solving steps that are quite complicated and overly academic; you need one or two rounds of follow-up questions before it helps you understand properly. If XJTLU AI could tailor to students' actual circumstances and provide more concise or efficient responses, I would try using XJTLU AI.
> CN：存在。比如说你要让它教你，你需要把课件导入进去，这个需要一点时间。然后外部 AI 一般的教学方法给你的解题步骤是比较复杂的、比较学术的，就是说你需要一到两句的追问，它才会帮助你更好地理解。如果说西浦的 AI 能够贴合学生实际情况，来做一些比较简洁的回复，或者更高效的回复的话，会尝试用西浦 AI。

**访谈员**
> EN：Understood. For external AI assisting your study in ADS401, on a scale of 1 to 5, how would you rate your overall satisfaction?
> CN：明白。你当前对于外部 AI 辅助你进行 ADS401 这节课的学习，总体的满意度如果从 1 到 5 分打分，大概能给到几分？

**被访谈者**
> EN：I'd give it around 4 out of 5.
> CN：可以给到 4 分左右。

**访谈员**
> EN：That's quite a high evaluation. Why did you give this score?
> CN：评价还是比较高的，为什么会给出这样的分数呢？

**被访谈者**
> EN：First, the answers are accurate. Second, through follow-up prompts, I can help myself understand the concepts, which achieves my goal. So I gave it a 4.
> CN：一个就是首先答案是准确的；其次我通过追问，能够帮助自己理解这个知识，达到我的目的了，所以给 4 分。

**访谈员**
> EN：Aside from accuracy, what other factors do you weigh when considering external AI?
> CN：除了准确性之外，对于外部 AI 的考虑，你还会衡量哪些方面？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Primarily accuracy; another is that its response speed is quite fast. Mainly speed and efficiency.
> CN：主要一个是准确性，另一个是它的响应速度也比较快。主要就是速度和效率。

**访谈员**
> EN：Suppose XJTLU currently had an AI teaching assistant capability embedded in platforms like Learning Mall, what capabilities would you hope this XJTLU AI to possess?
> CN：假设当前的西浦有一个类似于 AI 助教的能力，嵌入在类似于 Learning Mall 这样的平台里，你希望这个西浦 AI 具备什么样的能力？

**被访谈者**
> EN：First, the answers must be accurate. Second, its response speed needs to be fast—it shouldn't take too long to think. Third, if XJTLU has this TA, it should definitely be able to output the knowledge from course slides to us more effectively. If it can deliver better results than external AI, I will use it.
> CN：我觉得首先答案一定是准确的。其次它的响应速度需要快一点，不能说思考时间太长。再次的话，如果西浦有这个助教，它肯定是能把课件里的知识更好地输出给我们。如果它能比外部 AI 输出的效果更好的话，我会用。

**访谈员**
> EN：Another question: is your requirement for accuracy, authenticity, and response speed rooted in wanting XJTLU AI to have them, or is it because your current external AI already achieves this and you expect XJTLU AI to match it?
> CN：还有一个问题：你对于 AI 提供的真实性、准确性以及响应速度的需求，是来自于你希望西浦 AI 具备这些，还是说因为你现在使用的外部 AI 已经做到了这点，所以你希望西浦 AI 也能达到？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Speed and accuracy are what external AI already has; I hope XJTLU AI can possess these features too. Beyond that, if it could do even better tailored specifically to the course, that would be even better.
> CN：速度和准确性是外部 AI 已有的，我希望西浦 AI 也能具备这样的功能。在此基础上，它如果能针对课程做得更好就更好了。

**访谈员**
> EN：So because the external AI you currently use already possesses these capabilities, you hope XJTLU AI can have similar capabilities as well, right?
> CN：就是因为你在用现有的外部 AI 它已经具备了这样的能力，所以你也希望西浦 AI 能有类似能力，对吧？

**被访谈者**
> EN：Right.
> CN：对。

**访谈员**
> EN：Great, thank you so much! That wraps up our interview today. Thank you.
> CN：好的，谢谢！那我们本次的访谈就大概到这里，谢谢。

**被访谈者**
> EN：Great, thank you.
> CN：好，谢谢。
