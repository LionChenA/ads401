ADS401 Students' Awareness, Adoption and Use of XJTLU AI

This is a voluntary ADS401 coursework survey exploring students' awareness, adoption, and use of XJTLU AI.

"XJTLU AI" refers to any student-accessible AI agent service officially provided by XJTLU, including but not limited to Junmou AI and the XJTLU Library AI Portal.

"External AI" refers to any AI agent service not officially provided by XJTLU, including but not limited to ChatGPT, DeepSeek, and similar services accessed independently.

ADS401 Evaluation Methods and Statistics is an XJTLU module for students on the MSc Human-Computer Interaction and MSc Social Computing programmes, including part-time Social Computing students. Unless otherwise specified, answer about ADS401 learning in the past four weeks (28 days).

Participation is voluntary. This survey does not ask for identity or contact details unless you choose Yes to the optional follow-up interview invitation. If you choose Yes, the final question asks for an ID and an email address or telephone number so the research team can match your answers to a potential interview invitation; your responses will therefore not be anonymous to the research team.

Such information will be used only for interview recruitment and scheduling, accessed only by authorised study-team members, excluded from all reports and analysis datasets, and deleted within 30 days after all follow-up interviews are completed. You may decline an interview invitation later. Do not provide other sensitive information. Estimated completion time: 5–7 minutes.
