# ADS401 Students' Awareness, Adoption and Use of XJTLU AI

# ADS401 学生 XJTLU AI 认知、采纳与使用调查

## Introduction / 问卷说明

This is a voluntary ADS401 coursework survey exploring students' awareness, adoption, and use of XJTLU AI. / 这是一份自愿参与的 ADS401 课程调查，了解学生对西浦 AI 的认知、采纳与使用情况。

**"XJTLU AI"** refers to any student-accessible AI agent service officially provided by XJTLU, including but not limited to Junmou AI and the XJTLU Library AI Portal. / **「XJTLU AI」**指西浦官方提供、学生可用的 AI Agent 服务，包括但不限于君谋 AI 和西浦图书馆 AI Portal。

**"External AI"** refers to any AI agent service not officially provided by XJTLU, including but not limited to ChatGPT, DeepSeek, and similar services accessed independently. / **「External AI（外部 AI）」**指非西浦官方提供的 AI Agent 服务，包括但不限于独立使用的 ChatGPT、DeepSeek 等。

**ADS401 Evaluation Methods and Statistics** is an XJTLU module for students on the **MSc Human-Computer Interaction and MSc Social Computing programmes**. It teaches you to evaluate computer systems through experiments, surveys, interviews, and data analysis, and to communicate your findings using evidence. / **ADS401（评估方法与统计）**是西浦面向 **MSc 人机交互与 MSc 社会计算专业** 的模块，教你通过实验、问卷、访谈与数据分析评估计算机系统，并用证据表达结论。

Unless otherwise specified, answer about **ADS401 learning in the past four weeks (28 days)**. / 如无特别说明，请就**过去四周（28 天）内 ADS401 学习**作答。

Participation is voluntary. This survey does not ask for identity or contact details unless you choose Yes to the optional follow-up interview invitation (Q23). If you choose Yes, Q24 asks for an ID and an email address or telephone number so the research team can match your answers to a potential interview invitation; your responses will therefore not be anonymous to the research team. Such information will be used only for interview recruitment and scheduling, accessed only by authorised study-team members, excluded from all reports and analysis datasets, and deleted within 30 days after all follow-up interviews are completed. You may decline an interview invitation later. Do not provide other sensitive information. Estimated completion time: 5–7 minutes. / 参与自愿。本问卷不收集身份或联系方式，除非你在 Q23 选择愿意参加可选的后续访谈；届时 Q24 将收集 ID 与邮箱/电话，仅用于访谈邀请与安排，仅研究团队授权成员可访问，不进入任何报告与分析数据集，并将在全部访谈完成后 30 天内删除；你可随时拒绝访谈邀请。请勿提供其他敏感信息。预计用时 5–7 分钟。
