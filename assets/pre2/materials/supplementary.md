## Section 1: Participant Information Sheet & Voluntary Consent

### 1.1 Study Purpose & Nature
This voluntary survey forms part of an empirical Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) coursework evaluation in module ADS401 (*Evaluation Methods and Statistics*) at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU). The evaluation examines student awareness, adoption, recent academic usage, perceived usefulness, ease of use, trust, and adoption barriers regarding officially provided university AI services (primarily the XJTLU AI Agent Platform / Junmou AI, and Learning Mall assistants) in contrast with external commercial AI alternatives (e.g., ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Kimi).

### 1.2 Target Population & Voluntary Participation
- **Sampling Frame**: All full-time postgraduate students enrolled in ADS401 for the 2026–27 academic year ($N = 50$).
- **Exclusion**: The 4 student researchers conducting this study are excluded from the sampling pool, yielding 46 potentially eligible invitees.
- **Voluntary Nature**: Participation is completely voluntary. Respondents may exit the survey at any point prior to submission without penalty or consequence.

### 1.3 Anonymity, Data Protection & Course Grading Decoupling
- **No Identification in Survey Core**: The survey does not collect names, student ID numbers, IP addresses, or tracking cookies.
- **Safety Harbor Guarantee**: Survey responses are decoupled from module assessments, grading, and academic integrity scrutiny. Students' candid disclosure of AI usage in coursework will not affect their standing.
- **Optional Interview Follow-up**: Only respondents who voluntarily opt into the optional follow-up interview (Q20 = Yes) will be invited in Q21 to provide contact details (student ID and email/telephone). These contact records are stored strictly in an isolated, encrypted contact table separated from the survey response dataset and accessible only by authorised study team members for scheduling.
- **Repository Hygiene**: In strict compliance with the module coursework policy, **no raw participant records or identifiable personal data** are ever stored in public repositories or published on the project website.

### 1.4 Digital Consent Gate
Informed consent is formally administered at the entry gate:
- **Q1 [Eligibility]**: Verification of current enrollment in ADS401.
- **Q2 [Informed Consent]**: Explicit confirmation of having read the study information and voluntary agreement to participate. Selecting "No, I do not agree" immediately terminates the session.

## Section 3: Semi-Structured Interview Protocol

### 3.1 Methodological Context & Purpose
Following an explanatory sequential mixed-methods design (Ivankova et al., 2006; Fetters et al., 2013), semi-structured follow-up interviews are conducted after preliminary survey analysis. While the quantitative survey measures *what* tools students use and *how often*, the qualitative interviews investigate *why* students make specific adoption choices, uncover critical frustration incidents, and probe adoption barriers in depth.

### 3.2 Sampling Strategy & Participant Recruitment
- **Sampling Pool**: Recruited exclusively from survey respondents who voluntarily agreed to contact in Q20 and provided contact details in Q21.
- **Sample Size**: Approximately 4–6 students.
- **Purposive Quota Allocation**: Prioritises dual users (**Group 1: Both AI**) and commercial-only users (**Group 2: Other AI only**) to capture direct comparative trade-offs and institutional displacement mechanisms.
- **Duration**: 15–20 minutes per interview.

### 3.3 Core Interview Topics & Guiding Questions
1. **Tool-Choice Motivations & Workflow Context**:
   - "When beginning an ADS401 assignment, where do you typically start when seeking AI assistance (web browser, IDE, or campus portal)?"
   - "What factors prompt you to choose an external commercial AI over XJTLU's official Junmou AI platform?"
2. **Critical Incidents & Adoption Friction**:
   - "Can you recount a specific instance where Junmou AI was helpful or failed to meet your coursework needs?"
   - "What technical, cognitive, or accessibility hurdles have prevented you from using campus AI more regularly?"
3. **Verification Habits & Cognitive Scaffolding**:
   - "How do you verify the correctness and reliability of AI-generated answers before integrating them into coursework?"
   - "Do you find yourself using AI primarily to accelerate routine formatting/boilerplate, or to explore new concepts and debug errors?"
4. **Institutional Policy Perception & Recommendations**:
   - "How clear do you find university guidelines on permissible versus prohibited AI assistance across your modules?"
   - "What specific feature or policy improvements would make XJTLU AI genuinely indispensable for your daily learning?"

### 3.4 Qualitative Analysis & Triangulation
- **Thematic Analysis**: Audio recordings and transcripts are coded following the six-phase thematic analysis framework (Braun & Clarke, 2006) to identify recurring adoption themes and barriers.
- **Mixed-Methods Integration**: Qualitative themes are mapped directly against survey cross-tabulations and Likert ratings to provide an integrated explanation of adoption patterns.
