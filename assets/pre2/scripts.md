# ADS401 Students' Perspective on AI Agents at XJTLU

## Background

### Research Background
XJTLU provides AI services such as Junmou AI to support learning. However, ADS401 students’ awareness, adoption, and use remain unclear.
> 西浦提供君谋 AI 等服务以支持学习，但 ADS401 学生的认知、采纳及使用情况尚不明确。

### Research Motivation & Aim
This study explores ADS401 students’ use or non-use of XJTLU AI and the reasons behind their choices, to inform future improvements to these tools.
> 本研究探索 ADS401 学生使用或不使用 XJTLU AI 的情况及原因，为相关工具今后的优化迭代提供参考。

 *link: https://lionchena.github.io/ads401/*

## Research Questions
- RQ1: Awareness & Adoption
To what extent are students in our ADS401 module aware of and adopting XJTLU AI?
> 当前 ADS401 Module 的学生对 XJTLU AI 的认知和采纳程度如何？

- RQ2: Usage Patterns
How do students in ADS401 use XJTLU AI for their academic tasks?
> 当前 ADS401 Module 的学生如何在学习任务中使用 XJTLU AI？

- RQ3: Adoption Factors & Perceived Comparison
What perceived benefits, barriers, and differences between XJTLU AI and external AI services are associated with students' adoption and intentions to continue, try, or resume using XJTLU AI?
> XJTLU AI 与其他 AI 服务之间的哪些感知收益、使用障碍和差异，与学生采纳 XJTLU AI，以及继续、尝试或恢复使用的意愿有关？

*References — Perceived usefulness and technology acceptance: Davis (1989), [Perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and user acceptance of information technology](https://doi.org/10.2307/249008); student GenAI use and perceptions: Farinosi & Melchior (2025), [To adopt or to ban?](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-025-05982-7).*

## Research Methodology
Explanatory Sequential Mixed-Methods Design
> 解释性顺序混合方法研究设计

### Target Participants

For Module ADS401:

48 enrolled → 4 researchers excluded → 44 potentially eligible invitees → voluntary survey participation → achieved sample to be reported after collection. Cohort composition (per class roster): 43 PG1 mainland-China HCI students — including the 4 researchers — and 5 presumed non-mainland-China HCI students; the 44 invitees comprise 39 mainland and 5 non-mainland students.
> 48 名在读学生 → 排除 4 名研究者 → 44 名潜在受邀学生 → 自愿参与问卷 → 收集结束后报告实际样本量。人群构成（按班级名册）：PG1 中国大陆 HCI 学生 43 人（含 4 名研究者），推测非中国大陆 HCI 学生 5 人；44 名受邀者中大陆 39 人、非大陆 5 人。



### Survey Design
- Cross-sectional online questionnaire targeting ADS401 students.
> 面向 ADS401 学生开展横断面在线问卷调查。
- Planned grouping by reported AI use for ADS401 in the past four weeks:
    (Both AI / Other AI Only / No AI / XJTLU AI Only)
> 按过去四周 ADS401 学习中的 AI 使用情况预设四组：Both AI / Other AI Only / No AI / XJTLU AI Only；各组实际人数待收集后确认。
- Measures: **Awareness, Ever-use, Recent Academic Usage, Perceived Usefulness, Ease of Use, Trust, Barriers, and Future-use Intentions**.
> 测量认知、是否曾经使用、近期学习用途、感知有用性、易用性、信任、使用障碍及未来使用意愿。

- Various academic activity categories will be used for consistent comparison.
> 不同学术活动类别会用于一致比较。

Usefulness and ease-of-use items are treated as exploratory, context-specific measures rather than the complete validated TAM scales.
> 有用性与易用性条目作为本研究情境下的探索性测量，不等同于完整、经过验证的 TAM 量表。

*The Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) includes validated scales for perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use (Davis, 1989). These constructs will inform questionnaire items adapted to the XJTLU AI context.*

> *技术接受模型（TAM）包含经过验证的感知有用性与感知易用性量表（Davis, 1989）。本研究将参考这两个构念，设计适用于 XJTLU AI 情境的问卷题项。*

### Data Analysis
- Descriptive Statistics: Report counts and percentages for awareness, ever-use, and past-four-week usage, with the relevant response denominator for each item.
> 描述性统计：分别报告认知、是否曾经使用及过去四周使用情况的人数与比例，注明各题有效回答人数。
- Cross-tabulation: Compare usage patterns across four groups.
> 交叉分析：比较四组学生的使用模式。
- Rating Analysis: Summarise activity-specific usefulness, usefulness/ease-of-use/trust items, and future-use intentions descriptively; exclude N/A from rating summaries. Activity-category matrices cover categories A–E plus an Other row; Other-row responses are summarised separately (counts and ratings) and excluded from A–E paired comparisons.
> 评分分析：描述各活动的有用性、有用性／易用性／信任条目及未来使用意愿；N/A 不计入评分汇总。活动矩阵覆盖 A–E 五类及“其他”行；“其他”行单独汇总（计数与评分），不计入 A–E 配对比较。
- Perceived Comparison: Examine paired activity ratings only where dual users rated both tools for the same category; report direct comparison responses separately, including 'Unable to compare'.
> 感知比较：仅在双工具用户对同一活动类别均有有效评分时进行配对比较；直接比较题单独汇总，并报告“无法比较”。
- Frequency & verification items: “Can't recall” responses on the usage-frequency items (Q10/Q18a) are reported separately and excluded from band percentages; verification frequency (Q12) uses quantified anchors (never to every time, scaled per 10 uses), with “Unable to judge” reported separately.
> 频次与核实条目：使用频次题（Q10/Q18a）的“不记得了”单独报告，不计入档位百分比；核实频率（Q12）使用量化锚点（从不到每次，按每 10 次换算），“无法判断”单独报告。

*References — Explanatory sequential design: Ivankova et al. (2006), [Using mixed-methods sequential explanatory design: From theory to practice](https://doi.org/10.1177/1525822X05282260); questionnaire design resource (survey instrument): Chung et al. (2024), [Student perspectives on AI in Higher Education: Student Survey](https://doi.org/10.26180/27915930); conceptual basis for usefulness and ease of use (not validation of the adapted questionnaire): Davis (1989), [Perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and user acceptance of information technology](https://doi.org/10.2307/249008).*

## Follow-up Interviews & Integrated Analysis

### Semi-structured Interviews
- Sampling: Purposive sampling among survey respondents who opt into follow-up contact, informed by initial survey analysis.
> 抽样方法：初步分析问卷后，在同意后续联系的受访者中进行目的性抽样。
- Participants: about 4–6 students, **prioritising students in 2 groups (Both AI / Other AI Only)**.
> 参与者：约 4–6 名，重点关注使用两种 AI 和只使用其他 AI 分组的学生。
- Duration: 15–20 minutes per interview.
> 访谈时长：每人 15–20 分钟。
- Focus: Tool-choice motivations, adoption barriers, and learning experiences.
> 访谈重点：工具选择动机、采纳障碍与学习体验。

### Qualitative & Integrated Analysis
- Thematic Analysis: Code interviews and identify recurring themes.
> 主题分析：编码访谈数据，归纳重复出现的主题。
- Integration: Connect interview themes with survey patterns.
> 综合分析：将访谈主题与问卷结果相互对照。
- Outcome: Explain adoption decisions and identify potential XJTLU AI improvements.
> 研究产出：解释学生的采纳决策，并识别潜在 XJTLU AI 改进方向。

The survey identifies how students use different AI services, while follow-up interviews explore why they make those choices.
> 问卷帮助我们识别学生如何使用不同 AI 服务，而后续访谈进一步解释他们为什么作出这些选择。

### Limitations
- Single-module sample
The findings describe participating ADS401 students; voluntary participation may limit representativeness even within ADS401, and findings should not be generalised to all XJTLU students.
> 单一 Module 样本：结果描述参与研究的 ADS401 学生；自愿参与可能使样本在 ADS401 内部也缺乏代表性，不能推广至西浦全体学生。
- Self-reported data
The study relies mainly on students' reported behaviors and perceptions.
> 自我报告数据：研究主要依赖学生自述的使用行为和主观感知。

- Interview coverage
Prioritising Both AI and Other AI Only may underrepresent No AI and XJTLU AI Only perspectives.
> 访谈覆盖：重点访谈 Both AI 和 Other AI Only，可能使 No AI 和 XJTLU AI Only 的观点覆盖不足。

- Cross-sectional design
The survey captures recent use and future intentions, not causal effects or observed long-term continued use.
> 横断面设计：问卷记录近期使用与未来意愿，不能确定因果关系或实际长期持续使用。

- Perception-based comparison
Comparisons with external AI tools do not establish objective differences in system capabilities.
> 基于感知的比较：与外部 AI 工具的比较不能证明系统实际能力的客观差异。

*References — Questionnaire design resource (survey instrument): Chung et al. (2024), [Student perspectives on AI in Higher Education: Student Survey](https://doi.org/10.26180/27915930); student self-report and self-selection bias: Farinosi & Melchior (2025), [To adopt or to ban?](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-025-05982-7).*
