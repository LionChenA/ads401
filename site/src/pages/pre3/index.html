Research Focus & Questions

ADS401 Students' Perspective on AI Agents at XJTLU

XJTLU provides AI services such as Junmou AI to support learning, yet ADS401 students' awareness, adoption and use of these tools remain unclear. This study explores their use or non-use of XJTLU AI and the reasons behind their choices, to inform future improvements.

RQ1 · Awareness & Adoption

To what extent are students in our ADS401 module aware of and adopting XJTLU AI?

RQ2 · Usage Patterns

How do students in ADS401 use XJTLU AI for their academic tasks?

RQ3 · Adoption Factors & Perceived Comparison

What perceived benefits, barriers, and differences between XJTLU AI and external AI services are associated with students' adoption and intentions to continue, try, or resume using XJTLU AI?

Study Design & Data Collection

How we collected the evidence

A non-experimental, cross-sectional online survey followed by purposive interviews. Question references use the 27 numbered items in questionnaire v2. No conditions were assigned.

RQ1 · Awareness & Adoption

High awareness, limited recent adoption

A cross-sectional snapshot of awareness, prior use and recent academic engagement — different state slices, not a tracked conversion funnel. n = 27 submissions.

Q7 · Q8 · Q9 · Q10 — percentages use all 27 submissions. Q8 has one missing answer; a missing answer is not a “No”. Cross-sectional; states are not necessarily nested.

Students broadly know XJTLU AI, but only 8 report using it in the past four weeks.

RQ3 · Perceived Experience

At least 5 of 7 ratings are neutral or negative on every item

Eight recent XJTLU AI users (G1 + G4) answered; each statement has seven ratings and one N/A.

Each dot is one answer. 1 means strongly disagree; 3 is neutral; 5 means strongly agree. Median is the middle rating; N/A means no rating. Q14.

Efficiency and ease of use for ADS401 tasks have median 2; the other items have median 3. Small-sample descriptions, not TAM validation.
